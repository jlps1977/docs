# Trust Tier Framework

## Purpose

Defines operational trust tiers for users and providers participating in LUVO discovery, tourism, reviews, and commerce.

## Trust Tiers

### Tier 1
Basic participation tier.

### Tier 2
Operationally trusted tier.

### Tier 3
High-confidence trusted tier.

### Tier 4
Flagship trust tier.

## Trust Signals

- moderation stability
- review quality
- reservation consistency
- provider responsiveness
- tourism quality
- session consistency

## Visibility Impact

Higher trust tiers may influence:

- recommendation eligibility
- review publication confidence
- provider visibility
- tourism recommendation confidence

## Trust Safeguards

Trust systems must avoid:

- pay-to-trust behavior
- recommendation corruption
- visibility abuse
- tourism spam

## MVP Boundary

Trust tiers remain moderation-aware and operationally simple for MVP execution.
