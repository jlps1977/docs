# Provider Reputation Signals

## Purpose

Defines the operational signals used to understand provider quality, trust, reliability, and visibility eligibility inside LUVO.

## Core Principle

Trust protects discovery integrity, provider quality, review reliability, tourism quality, marketplace safety, and recommendation reliability.

## Provider Reputation Signals

- profile completeness
- operational responsiveness
- listing freshness
- review consistency
- reservation reliability
- content quality
- tourism suitability
- moderation stability
- sponsored visibility conduct

## Signal Categories

### Quality Signals
Content completeness, media quality, category accuracy, and editorial fit.

### Reliability Signals
Response timing, reservation handling, order handling, and operational consistency.

### Trust Signals
Moderation history, review integrity, suspicious behavior absence, and marketplace conduct.

### Tourism Signals
Visitor friendliness, location clarity, experience quality, and local relevance.

## MVP Boundary

Provider reputation remains operational and moderation-aware. Advanced ML scoring and enterprise compliance systems are out of scope.
