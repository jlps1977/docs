# Marketplace Engine Product Sheet

## Objective

The Marketplace Discovery Engine enables users to discover local restaurants, providers, places, offers, menus, experiences and tourism-relevant locations through structured, AI-enriched local data.

## Problem Solved

Local discovery is fragmented across maps, social media, delivery apps, review sites and informal recommendations. LUVO consolidates discovery into a city-native platform with actionable local intent: discover, order, reserve, visit and experience.

## Target Users

- Local consumers
- Tourists
- Restaurants
- Service providers
- Hotels and tourism operators
- Advertisers
- Internal LUVO operators

## Core Capabilities

- business/place discovery
- category and subcategory navigation
- geospatial browsing
- provider profile pages
- menu/content enrichment
- review and rating support
- sponsored placements
- tourism-aware ranking
- editorial/insider curation
- AI-generated tags and descriptions

## Non-Goals

The Marketplace Engine does not own payment settlement, delivery dispatch, ad billing or reservation inventory. It integrates with those systems.

## Business Value

Marketplace discovery creates the demand surface for all LUVO monetization layers:

- provider subscriptions
- sponsored listings
- ads
- delivery commission
- reservation monetization
- tourism packages
- data/analytics products

## Dependencies

- GEO Authority Engine
- AI Enrichment Engine
- Provider System
- Ads Engine
- Tourism Engine
- Analytics Layer

## KPIs

- search-to-profile-view rate
- profile-view-to-action rate
- sponsored CTR
- provider activation rate
- category coverage
- geodata completeness
- AI enrichment accuracy
- repeat discovery sessions

## Risks

- duplicate provider records
- low-quality AI enrichment
- ranking bias
- poor geodata boundaries
- SEO fragmentation
- unclear ownership between marketplace and tourism modules
