# Ads Engine Product Sheet

## Objective

The Ads Engine monetizes LUVO demand by allowing providers and advertisers to purchase sponsored visibility across discovery, tourism, marketplace, restaurant and provider surfaces.

## Problem Solved

Local businesses need measurable digital demand generation but lack simple city-native ad tools. LUVO Ads provides context-aware placements based on location, user intent, category, tourism relevance and provider opportunities.

## Core Capabilities

- campaign creation
- sponsored listings
- sponsored filters
- impression tracking
- interaction tracking
- campaign status management
- geo-scoped delivery
- provider opportunity mapping
- basic campaign analytics
- future bidding and budget optimization

## Monetization Logic

Initial monetization should support:

- fixed campaign fees
- promoted placement packages
- provider dashboard upsells
- tourism category sponsorship
- future CPC/CPM/hybrid models

## Placement Surfaces

- marketplace search
- provider profile recommendations
- tourism guide pages
- restaurant discovery
- offers/deals sections
- category pages
- geographic landing pages

## Dependencies

- Marketplace Engine
- Provider System
- GEO Authority Engine
- Analytics Layer
- AI Enrichment Engine

## KPIs

- ad revenue as percentage of platform revenue
- gross margin
- impressions
- CTR
- sponsored placement conversion
- campaign renewal rate
- provider ROAS proxy
- fill rate by category/geography

## Risks

- weak attribution
- poor provider understanding of ad value
- oversaturation of sponsored content
- low inventory quality
- ad fraud or invalid impressions
- excessive dependence on manual campaign ops

## Expansion

Future versions may include:

- auction-based ranking
- RTB-like internal bidding
- advertiser self-serve dashboard
- automated budget recommendations
- AI-generated ad copy
- cross-platform campaign intelligence
