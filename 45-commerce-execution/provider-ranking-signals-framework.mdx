# Provider Ranking Signals Framework

## Purpose

Defines how provider visibility is influenced while preserving editorial integrity, semantic discovery quality, and tourism trust.

## Ranking Inputs

- semantic relevance
- listing quality
- freshness
- tourism alignment
- engagement consistency
- operational responsiveness
- hidden gem preservation
- sponsored visibility eligibility

## Ranking Safeguards

The ranking system must prevent:

- pay-to-win discovery
- low-quality sponsored dominance
- repetitive visibility loops
- tourism spam
- semantic degradation

## Visibility Tiers

### Tier 1
High-confidence flagship providers.

### Tier 2
Operationally strong providers.

### Tier 3
Exploratory or newly onboarded providers.

## Sponsored Influence Boundaries

Sponsored visibility may affect dedicated sponsored surfaces but must not override semantic relevance or editorial protection layers.
