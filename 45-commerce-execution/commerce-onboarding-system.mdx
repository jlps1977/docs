# Commerce Onboarding System

## Purpose

Defines onboarding standards for providers, operators, moderators, and commerce support personnel participating in LUVO Commerce Execution.

## Onboarding Objectives

- operational consistency
- marketplace trust preservation
- tourism quality preservation
- semantic discovery protection
- sponsored visibility governance

## Provider Onboarding Flow

```mermaid
flowchart LR
A[Provider Application] --> B[Profile Completion]
B --> C[Operational Review]
C --> D[Approval State]
D --> E[Marketplace Activation]
```

## Required Operational Areas

Providers must understand:

- listing quality expectations
- tourism experience quality
- availability management
- reservation management
- sponsored visibility boundaries
- marketplace trust expectations

## Operational Education Topics

- discovery quality
- semantic metadata quality
- tourism friendliness
- provider trust standards
- anti-spam rules
- moderation escalation paths

## Commerce Maturity Tiers

### Tier 1
New providers under observation.

### Tier 2
Operationally stable providers.

### Tier 3
Trusted flagship providers.

## MVP Boundary

The onboarding system is intentionally lightweight, operationally simple, and optimized for marketplace growth without enterprise operational overhead.
