# Tourism Commerce Framework

## Purpose

Defines how LUVO operationalizes tourism-oriented commerce flows while preserving semantic discovery quality and local exploration integrity.

## Tourism Commerce Scope

- tourism reservations
- local experience providers
- attraction discovery
- experience monetization
- itinerary commerce surfaces
- tourism-oriented promoted discovery

## Tourism Commerce Lifecycle

```mermaid
flowchart LR
A[Tourism Discovery] --> B[Contextual Exploration]
B --> C[Commerce Intent]
C --> D[Reservation or Action]
D --> E[Operational Tracking]
```

## Commerce Categories

- restaurants
- rooftop venues
- beach clubs
- tours
- cenote experiences
- nightlife experiences
- wellness experiences
- cultural activities

## Tourism Safeguards

The system must avoid:

- tourism spam
- low-quality promoted experiences
- misleading local discovery
- over-commercialization of discovery flows

## Discovery Preservation

Tourism monetization must preserve:

- hidden gem visibility
- editorial quality
- contextual discovery quality
- authentic local exploration

## MVP Assumptions

Tourism commerce remains lightweight and marketplace-oriented during MVP execution.
