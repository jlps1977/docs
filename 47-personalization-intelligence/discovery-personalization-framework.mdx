# Discovery Personalization Framework

## Purpose

Defines operational personalization rules for contextual discovery, tourism exploration, and recommendation adaptation.

## Personalization Inputs

- session activity
- nearby exploration
- category affinity
- vibe affinity
- tourism intent
- saved places
- exploration continuity

## Personalization Rules

- prioritize exploration diversity
- preserve hidden gems
- rotate recommendation pools
- avoid repetitive surfacing
- preserve contextual freshness


## Operational Constraints

The MVP avoids realtime AI inference and relies on metadata-driven heuristics and lightweight scoring.
